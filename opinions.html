Editorial by Ysabella Samantha M. Azura | STE 9-Justice| 03/11/2026

One of the most pressing problems in our community today is improper waste disposal. Many residents continue to throw trash in streets, canals, and empty lots, which leads to pollution and flooding during heavy rains. I strongly believe this issue deserves immediate attention because it affects the health and safety of everyone in the community. When garbage accumulates, it attracts pests and spreads diseases. If this problem continues, it will only worsen the living conditions of residents, especially children and the elderly.

This problem often happens because of a lack of discipline and awareness about proper waste management. Some people do not segregate their waste or simply ignore disposal rules. To address this issue, local officials should strengthen waste management programs and ensure regular garbage collection. Schools can also educate students about environmental responsibility so that they grow up practicing proper waste disposal. At the same time, community members must cooperate by reducing plastic use, segregating trash, and participating in clean-up drives.

Solving this issue requires cooperation from both the government and the community. Change will not happen overnight, but it can start with small actions from each individual. When residents become more responsible for their waste, the community can become cleaner and safer for everyone. By working together and raising awareness, we can protect our environment and create a healthier place for future generations

"t is time for us to stand together and demand the change our community deserves."
