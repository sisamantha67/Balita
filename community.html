Highlighting issues and events that affect our society
Top 5 Community Issues
  1. Flooding in Low-lying Areas
    Flooding occurs when heavy rainfall or poor drainage causes water to overwhelm streets, homes, and public spaces. It destroys property, spreads waterborne diseases, and displaces families, putting the entire community at risk.
  2. Lack of Street Lighting
    The absence of adequate lighting leaves roads and public spaces dangerously dark during nighttime hours. This encourages criminal activity, increases accidents, and makes residents — especially women and children — feel unsafe in their own neighborhood.
  3. Garbage Disposal Problems
    Poor garbage disposal results from the failure to regularly collect and manage waste, leaving litter and overflowing bins scattered across the community. Unmanaged waste spreads disease, attracts pests, pollutes the environment, and degrades the overall quality of life for residents.
  4. Youth Unemployment
    Youth unemployment is the widespread inability of young people to find meaningful work despite being of working age and willing to contribute. It traps youth in poverty, fuels crime and social unrest, and robs the community of its greatest resource for future growth.
  5. Poor Road Conditions
    Poor road conditions refer to the deterioration of streets through potholes, cracks, and a lack of basic maintenance. Bad roads cause accidents, damage vehicles, slow economic activity, and signal to residents that their community is being neglected by those in power.

🏛️ Historical Fact:
Barangay San Isidro is one of the seven official barangays of Cainta, Rizal, a municipality considered one of the oldest in Luzon, founded on August 15, 1571. (Expedia) The barangay is named after San Isidro Labrador (Saint Isidore the Farmer), the patron saint of farmers, reflecting the area's agricultural roots in earlier times.
