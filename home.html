Stray Animals in Cainta—being put up for adoption

People wonder where these pets will go—will they stay on the shelters, or will they find their true loving families

Many stray animals in Cainta are being rescued and placed up for adoption to give them a second chance at a safe and loving home. Animal welfare groups and volunteers help care for these animals by providing food, medical attention, and temporary shelter. By adopting instead of buying pets, people can help reduce the number of animals living on the streets. Adoption also gives these stray animals the opportunity to experience kindness, safety, and proper care.
